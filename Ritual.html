Stormy ritual • 10 minutes

Repair the moment before you try to solve the problem.

This page is where mainstream users should feel the value immediately. The language stays grounded, while the structure quietly borrows from tantric principles: presence, breath, eye contact, intentional touch, and shared attention.

1

Arrive

Sit facing each other. Place one hand on your own chest and one hand on your partner’s arm. Take five slow breaths together without speaking.

2 min
2

Name what is true

Each person finishes one sentence: “Right now, I feel…” The other person can only repeat back what they heard. No fixing, defending, or explaining yet.

3 min
3

Reconnect through touch

Hold hands or rest knee-to-knee. Ask: “What would help you feel safe with me in this moment?” Choose one small action and do it immediately.

3 min
4

Close the loop

End with one appreciation and one intention for the next 24 hours. This turns the ritual into a habit, not just a one-time emotional rescue.
