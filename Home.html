Positioning for conversion

Lead with outcomes people already want.

To maximize subscriptions, the product should sell emotional results in everyday language: reconnect faster, argue better, feel wanted again, and build a daily habit together.

1. Low-friction entry

Start with mood, not philosophy

Ask what the couple needs right now: calm, repair, intimacy, playfulness, or reconnection. This removes the fear that the app is “too spiritual” before value is felt.

2. Clear benefit

Frame rituals as guided connection exercises

Keep “tantric” present as a deeper layer, but let the first promise be practical and universal: less distance, more presence, better repair after conflict.

3. Subscription trigger

Create a repeatable evening habit

Subscriptions grow when the app becomes part of a couple’s rhythm. Offer daily plans, streak-friendly reminders, and weekly “reset night” rituals worth coming back for.

Messaging shift

Speak to skeptics and seekers at the same time

Use “connection ritual” first Offer “deeper practice” second Reduce incense-and-cosmos cues

Your home page should feel like a premium relationship wellness product, not a niche spiritual app. Keep the experience soft, sensual, and grounded through language like reset, closeness, presence, touch, and repair.

Membership structure

What earns the subscription

Free: one daily check-in, one repair ritual, one intimacy ritual.
Premium: adaptive ritual library, partner sync, weekly reconnection journeys, voice-led sessions, and post-conflict pathways.
Annual hook: “A private relationship gym for two” with progress arcs and seasonal plans.
